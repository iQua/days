Two research lines share this repository: **LeanGuard**, the trace-certificate
methodology built on the legacy engine, and the **exact executor** (Days AGO),
whose execution model is itself proved in Lean and whose backends are held to
byte-identity. This page summarizes both, and is careful to separate shipped
code, committed fixtures, and external campaign evidence.

## LeanGuard: trace certificates + trusted replay

![LeanGuard overview: the simulator emits a per-transition trace certificate and a trusted Lean checker replays it.](/figures/leanguard-intro.svg)

LeanGuard follows an "untrusted producer / trusted checker" workflow:

1. **Days** emits a *per-transition* trace (one row per protocol transition),
   including a deterministic snapshot of post-state.
2. A **Lean 4 checker** canonicalizes the trace — legacy protocol families
   sort by `(time_ns, event_id)` and reject duplicates; exact-engine (P10c)
   checkers key on the full `EventKey` and some require already-strict
   canonical order.
3. The checker **replays** an executable reference model and returns
   `ACCEPT` or `REJECT` with the first counterexample row.

See [/docs/verification/design](/docs/verification/design) for the practical
guide, [/docs/research/design](/docs/research/design) for the design notes,
and [/docs/reference/traces/schema](/docs/reference/traces/schema) for row
conventions. The coverage-guided test-generation loop that builds on these
checkers is summarized in
[/docs/research/test-generation](/docs/research/test-generation).

## The exact executor: proofs down, byte-identity across

The exact engine's claim is structural: five Lean theorems about the
execution machinery (`lean/DaysExecutor*`) license safe-horizon rounds and
intra-round reordering, and the shipped corpus gate then holds every backend
to the theorems' promise — the CPU pool and each compiled device backend
must reproduce the global Scalar reference's complete result byte-for-byte
(device-omitted diagnostics excluded) on every corpus fixture.

The device arc that made this practical at scale shipped in this tree:
planner equality across backends, persistent arenas, per-entity capacity
derivation with refuse-or-run faults and bounded retry, ring and vector
sizing, compact readback (only live regions return), and an opt-in capacity
warm start that affects sizing only. The largest committed fixture runs
**262,144 closed-loop TCP flows**
(`configs/benchmarks/p11/rq9_frontier_closed_k32.toml`); campaign evidence
records its complete state byte-identical across the two CUDA architectures
the project measures on.

Recent shipped work also includes: the exact TCP corpus/device gate (T24),
CUDA upload provisioning for unified-memory hosts (T27), strict legacy
configuration validation with exact fixture expression (T28), and
TOML-configurable `csv_logging` (T30).

## Runnable benchmarks and indicative figures

The committed E5 fixture family — 8,192 byte-terminated TCP flows on a k32
fat tree, Reno primary (`q200`), CUBIC, and a `q256` backup — is the
recommended runnable benchmark set. Indicative native run-clock medians on
one machine (`boston`: Intel i7-13700K, RTX 4090): **Scalar 126.2 s, CPU
pool at 22 workers 5.007 s, CUDA 0.759 s** on the Reno fixture. These are
machine-qualified measurements, not cross-platform guarantees.

## Measured negatives, kept on the record

Two device optimizations were implemented, measured in paired A/B campaigns,
and **not merged** — recorded here because the method is the point:

- **Pinned staging (T31)**: exactness passed; process wall regressed
  materially on the discrete-GPU host. Verdict: hold.
- **Structure-of-arrays drain layout**: all seven identity anchors byte-exact;
  slower on the E5 and every E6 device clock in the paired A/B (one process
  wall at parity). Verdict: the shipped layout stands.

## External campaign evidence

The E6 UDP/CBR fixture family and the multi-engine comparison campaigns
(against sequential and MPI ns-3, Unison, GeDES, and the legacy engine) are
recorded in the project's evidence archive with machine-qualified results;
the E6 fixtures are not committed in this tree and are labeled external
until imported. Results quoted from those campaigns carry their machine and
clock definitions at the citation site.
